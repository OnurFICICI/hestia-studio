# Community code of conduct

Everyone is welcome to use, learn from, and contribute to Hestia Studio, regardless of background or experience.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, patient, and constructive. Critique ideas and code, not people.
- Help newcomers and explain feedback clearly.
- Respect other people's time; provide context and keep discussions relevant.
- Respect privacy. Share fictional examples and sanitized screenshots.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory abuse, sexual harassment, deliberate disruption, and publishing someone else's private information are not acceptable.

## Scope and moderation

These expectations apply to this repository's issues, pull requests, discussions, and other project spaces. Maintainers may edit or remove inappropriate content, lock conversations, or restrict participation when needed to protect the community. Responses should be proportionate and take context into account.

Use GitHub's **Report content** option on abusive content to report it privately to GitHub. Avoid repeating private information or escalating a dispute in public. Security vulnerabilities follow the separate [security policy](SECURITY.md).
